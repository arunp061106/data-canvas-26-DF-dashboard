Tiruppur shines in bright textiles,
the catastrophe is underground.

Known globally as the "Knitwear Capital of India", Tiruppur powers billions in textile exports. Yet decades of intense dyeing, bleaching, and chemical processing have burdened the fragile Noyyal River basin. Over-extraction and historic effluent leaching have driven groundwater levels down into hard rock aquifers while accelerating mineralization and salinity.

"Borewells families depend upon for daily survival are falling and getting saltier. We synthesize official CGWB Yearbooks, India-WRIS GPRS telemetry, and NASA POWER climate data to separate seasonal fluctuations from chronic systemic stress, pinpointing exactly where follow-up testing must be prioritized."

Scientific Integrity Disclosure: Multi-Year Salinity Trend Claim Boundary

Notice on Trend Claims: Claims that "TDS is steadily rising over multi-year periods" require repeated samples from identical well coordinates across matching seasons over consecutive years. While the 2024 national report provides robust Pre-Monsoon and Post-Monsoon samples for Tiruppur, Tiruppur district monitoring stations were not included in the published CGWB 2023 national quality sample tables (only Vellitirupur in neighbouring Erode appeared). Therefore, the 2024 chemical data is rigorously presented as a verified seasonal snapshot illustrating acute spatial salinity and post-monsoon ionic concentrations, rather than an unverified multi-year statistical trend.

Methodological Clarification: Gridded Satellite Estimates & Global Climate Indices

1. Precipitation Source: The rainfall time series represents NASA POWER satellite-reanalysis (MERRA-2) corrected precipitation for the single coordinate centroid of Tiruppur city (11.1085°N, 77.3411°E). It provides consistent meteorological context for urban and near-urban wells, but is not an official IMD district-wide rain gauge network total.
2. ENSO / RONI Teleconnection: Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI) anomalies track equatorial Pacific sea surface temperature variations. While El Niño conditions historically suppress peninsular Indian monsoon rainfall, RONI is presented as macro-climate background and must not be cited as standalone mechanical proof that global El Niño caused specific local well declines.

Methodological Guardrails & Adherence to Evidence Rules

  • No Imputed or Fabricated Data: No missing observations were filled, interpolated, or fabricated. Stations with missing seasonal readings are explicitly displayed as gaps or missing in records.
  • Rigorous Distinction of Units: Groundwater levels are strictly documented as Depth to Water Level in meters below ground level (m bgl). Values are not conflated with water table elevation above mean sea level (m amsl).
  • No False Multi-Year Salinity Claims: Because the 2023 CGWB national water quality report omitted Tiruppur monitoring stations, no claims of a multi-year "rising TDS trend" between 2023 and 2024 are made. Salinity is presented strictly as a 2024 seasonal snapshot (Pre vs Post-Monsoon).
  • Gridded vs Gauge Distinction: NASA POWER precipitation data is clearly identified as a single-coordinate gridded estimate, not an official district-wide rain gauge network aggregate.
  • ENSO Context vs Causality: NOAA RONI indices are shown as global oceanic-atmospheric climate backdrop, not direct proof of localized aquifer drawdown causation.
  • Spatial Boundary Integrity: Because the municipal budget PDF is a static raster representation without authoritative polygon geometries, stations are mapped by surveyed GPS coordinates rather than artificial ward boundary joins.

About "A Catastrophe behind Colours"

An investigative data analytics dashboard for Tiruppur and the Noyyal River basin. Integrating official groundwater monitoring records, chemical water quality laboratory analyses, and satellite precipitation series to protect community water access and advance UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation; SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being).

Core Data Custodians

• Central Ground Water Board (CGWB)
• National Water Informatics Centre (NWIC)
• NASA POWER Project (MERRA-2)
• NOAA Climate Prediction Center (CPC)
