2. Three-Pillar Priority Scoring Methodology

To identify which wells fail first during drought conditions, stations are ranked on a normalized 0–100 multi-criteria risk score evaluated across three hydrogeological pillars:

  • Pillar 1: Maximum Drawdown Depth (35% weight): Evaluates baseline aquifer depletion. Wells with water tables dropping beyond 20 m bgl receive 35 points; 10–20 m bgl receives 20 points; <10 m bgl receives 10 points.
  • Pillar 2: Seasonal Fluctuation Amplitude (35% weight): Measures aquifer storage buffer and vulnerability to dry-season collapse. Seasonal swings exceeding 5.0 m receive 35 points; 2.5–5.0 m receives 20 points; <2.5 m receives 10 points.
  • Pillar 3: Chemical Salinity & TDS (30% weight): Assesses chemical potability and wastewater intrusion. TDS >2,000 mg/L (exceeding BIS 10500 permissible limit) receives 30 points; 1,000–2,000 mg/L receives 18 points; <1,000 mg/L receives 8 points. If quality was not sampled, a neutral baseline of 10 points is applied with explicit disclosure.

Tier Classifications: High Priority (≥70 points, Red Alert), Medium Priority (50–69 points, Amber), Routine Monitoring (<50 points, Green).

3. Hydrogeological Limitations & Disclosures

Sign Convention Disclosure: Depth to water level is reported in meters below ground level (m bgl). Values increase as the water table falls deeper. In automated GPRS telemetry (India-WRIS), raw records are recorded as negative surface offsets (e.g., -5.51 m); these have been converted to positive depth below ground level (5.51 m bgl) per CGWB hydrogeological standards.

Omission of Hand-Drawn River Vector: No official GeoJSON vector line for the Noyyal River channel was included in the repository downloads. Hand-drawing geometric shapes is strictly prohibited by data integrity rules; therefore, the basemap uses genuine OpenStreetMap physical hydrography.

Quality Publication Frequency: The CGWB 2023 National Quality Report omitted Tiruppur district stations (only Vellitirupur in neighbouring Erode appears). Hence, multi-year cross-season quality comparisons are not claimed without official records.

Meteorological Context: Rainfall figures represent NASA POWER satellite-assimilated point estimates at the municipal centroid, not an official district-wide rain gauge network average.

Hydrogeological Footnote on Sign Convention: Depth to water level is strictly measured as meters below ground level (m bgl). A higher numerical value represents a deeper, more depleted water table. Negative telemetry sensor records have been converted to standard positive depth below surface. Modelled values are clearly designated with a [SCENARIO] badge.
