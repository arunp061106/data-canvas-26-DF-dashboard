📐 Three-Pillar Scoring Methodology (40 / 40 / 20)

Every borewell and dug well in the monitoring registry is scored on a transparent 100-point scale designed to isolate wells facing imminent dry-year failure:

Pillar Weight Measurement Metric Scoring Cut-offs
Pillar 1: Absolute Depth 40% (40 pts) Maximum depth to water level (m bgl) ≥20 m = 40 pts • 10–20 m = 25 pts • <10 m = 12 pts
Pillar 2: Seasonal Fluctuation 40% (40 pts) Pre-monsoon to post-monsoon drawdown swing (m) ≥5.0 m = 40 pts • 2.5–5.0 m = 25 pts • <2.5 m = 12 pts
Pillar 3: Salinity Stress 20% (20 pts) Total Dissolved Solids (mg/L) ≥2,000 mg/L = 20 pts • 1,000–2,000 mg/L = 12 pts • <1,000 mg/L = 6 pts

Tier Classification: High Priority (≥70 points, Red), Medium Priority (50–69 points, Amber), Routine Monitoring (<50 points, Green).

🛡️ Hydrogeological Disclosures & Cartographic Integrity

1. Schematic Transect Line: The dashed station transect line on both maps connects monitoring stations strictly in the order of their longitude (West to East). It provides spatial continuity across the basin. It is explicitly a schematic station transect, not the river's course, as no verified vector GeoJSON line exists in official public CGWB repository files.

2. Crystalline Basement Hydrogeology: Tiruppur sits upon weathered fissured crystalline rocks (charnockites and gneisses) where groundwater occurs under unconfined to semi-confined conditions. Specific yield ranges between 1.5% and 3%, causing severe drawdown during dry intervals.

3. Surveillance Gaps: Of 32 monitored stations in the 2023–24 baseline, only 9 core urban wells were resurveyed in the 2024–25 yearbook. Unmeasured stations are labeled "Not yet measured" rather than extrapolated.

Hydrogeological Footnote & Units: Depth to water level is reported in meters below ground level (m bgl). Values increase as the water table falls deeper. In automated GPRS telemetry (India-WRIS), raw records are recorded as negative surface offsets (e.g., -5.51 m); these have been converted to positive depth below ground level (5.51 m bgl) per CGWB hydrogeological standards. Salinity is expressed as Total Dissolved Solids (mg/L). Rainfall totals are expressed in millimeters (mm) calculated by summing daily NASA POWER MERRA-2 point precipitation estimates at 11.1085°N, 77.3411°E. Any modeled values are clearly identified with a [SCENARIO] badge.
