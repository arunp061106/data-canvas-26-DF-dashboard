Methodology: Three-Pillar Scoring Criteria (40 / 40 / 20)

Every borewell and dug well in the monitoring registry is scored on a transparent 100-point scale designed to identify stations facing critical failure during dry seasons:

Pillar Weight Measurement Metric Scoring Criteria
Pillar 1: Water Table Depth 40% (40 pts) Maximum depth to water level (m bgl) ≥20 m = 40 pts • 10–20 m = 25 pts • <10 m = 12 pts
Pillar 2: Salinity Stress 40% (40 pts) Total Dissolved Solids (mg/L) ≥2,000 mg/L = 40 pts • 1,200–2,000 mg/L = 25 pts • 500–1,200 mg/L = 12 pts • Not tested = 10 pts
Pillar 3: Seasonal Fluctuation 20% (20 pts) Pre-monsoon to post-monsoon drawdown swing (m) ≥5.0 m = 20 pts • 2.0–5.0 m = 10 pts • <2.0 m = 5 pts

Tier Classification: High Priority (≥70 points, Red), Medium Priority (50–69 points, Amber), Routine Monitoring (<50 points, Green).

Hydrogeological Disclosures & Geospatial Mapping Integrity

1. Geospatial Mapping Integrity: Basemap uses OpenStreetMap tiles with dark contrast filter applied strictly to the tile pane. All hand-drawn or schematic river lines have been removed to preserve geospatial integrity.

2. Crystalline Hydrogeology: Tiruppur rests on weathered and fissured crystalline rocks (gneisses and charnockites) with low primary porosity (specific yield typically 1.5% to 3.0%), making groundwater tables acutely vulnerable to deficit recharge during drought intervals.

3. Surveillance Gaps: Of 32 stations in the 2023–24 baseline network, only 9 core urban wells were resurveyed in the 2024–25 yearbook. Unmeasured stations are labeled "Not yet measured" rather than extrapolated.
